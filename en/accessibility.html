Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Medicare is committed to providing equal and accessible service and works to make its website and digital services usable by people with disabilities.

Website accessibility

The website was designed with reference to Israel's accessibility regulations, Israeli Standard 5568 and WCAG Level AA principles.

Measures implemented

Keyboard navigation, semantic headings, alternative text for informative images, improved contrast, a skip-to-content link, visible focus and screen-reader-friendly structure.

Accessibility feedback

If you encounter an accessibility difficulty, contact customer service at 08-6604455 or PHARMACY@MEDICARE.CO.IL.

Physical accessibility

For current information about physical accessibility at the service location, please contact customer service before arrival.

Before commercial publication, a full manual accessibility audit on the production server is recommended, including keyboard and screen-reader testing.
