Medicare

Medication sourcing and import from around the world

Medicare works with an extensive network of suppliers in the United States, Europe, Australia and additional markets to source medications unavailable in Israel and assess import options for the patient.

Core capability

Sourcing, import, pharmacy and logistics in one process

The service does not end with finding a supplier. Medicare coordinates the request through to delivery of the medication to the patient.

Global sourcing

Checking supply sources through an international supplier network.

Pathway review

Reviewing documentation and requirements relevant to the medication and the individual case.

Import & logistics

Handling import to Israel and required transport conditions.

Who is this service for?

Private patients

When a prescribed medication is not covered or not available in Israel.

Insurance companies / insured members

When a special medication or an international sourcing solution is required.

International patients

When medication is required during medical treatment in Israel.

No delivery-time commitment is made on this website. Availability and timing depend on the medication, supply source, documentation and applicable requirements.

The information on this website is general and does not replace medical or pharmacist advice. Medication supply and services are subject to applicable law, prescriptions and required approvals.
