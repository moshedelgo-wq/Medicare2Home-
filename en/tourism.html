Medicare

Medical Tourism – The treatment is in Israel. The solution can come from anywhere in the world.

Medicare supports patients coming to Israel for medical treatment and the professionals assisting them — from medication sourcing and quotation, through supply during treatment and hospitalization, to medications needed for continued care after returning home.

International inquiryFAQ
Who the service is for

One point of contact for the patient's medication needs in Israel

Medication needs can be coordinated through Medicare instead of searching for a separate solution at every stage.

Patients from abroad

Patients coming to Israel for surgery, oncology care, ongoing treatment or other medical care who need medications during their stay.

Medical tourism agencies

A professional contact for availability checks, quotations, supply and pharmacist guidance for agency patients.

Hospitals & international partners

Support for international patients, including cases where the required medication is unavailable in Israel.

International sourcing

Even when the medication is unavailable in Israel

Medicare has an extensive supplier network in the United States, Europe, Australia and additional markets. We explore sourcing and purchasing options abroad and can pursue import according to the applicable pathway and requirements.

From prescription to medication

1

Send prescription and request

Medication name, dose, quantity and patient details.

2

Availability check and sourcing

Medicare checks availability in Israel and abroad when needed.

3

Quotation and payment

A quotation is sent; once approved and payment is arranged, the order is processed.

4

Coordinated delivery

Delivery to the hotel, hospital, medical center or medical-tourism agency, as coordinated.

Before, during and after treatment

Before arrival

A prescription and request can be sent in advance so medication needs can be reviewed before arrival.

During hospitalization

Coordination and medication supply according to the patient’s needs and treatment location.

After discharge and before returning home

Continued supply to the place of stay and medications needed for continued treatment after leaving Israel, according to the prescription and request.

Customer service and pharmacists in three languages
Medical-tourism agents, patients and healthcare professionals can receive service and pharmacist guidance in Hebrew, English and Russian. Guidance is provided within the pharmacist’s professional scope and does not replace the treating physician.

The information on this website is general and does not replace medical or pharmacist advice. Medication supply and services are subject to applicable law, prescriptions and required approvals.
