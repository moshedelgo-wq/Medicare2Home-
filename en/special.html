Medicare

Have you received a prescription for a non-covered medication?

Medicare assists patients who have received a prescription for a medication outside standard coverage. If insurance coverage exists, we can work directly with the insurer; without coverage, private purchase may be possible. When the medication is unavailable in Israel, Medicare can source and import it specifically for the patient.

Send a prescription for reviewFAQ

Private purchase

A patient without insurance coverage can contact Medicare with the prescription and explore private purchase.

Insurance coverage

When coverage exists, Medicare works directly with the insurance company and handles the order after financial authorization.

Not available in Israel

Medicare can explore international sourcing and import options in accordance with applicable requirements.
