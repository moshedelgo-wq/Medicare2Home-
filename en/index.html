Who we help

The solution begins and ends
with the patient's need.

When a medication is not covered, unavailable in Israel or requires special handling, Medicare examines the right way to source it and deliver it to the patient.

Private patients

For prescriptions outside the standard coverage pathway, Medicare checks availability, purchasing options and insurance coverage and can source abroad when needed.

Insured members

Medicare works directly with insurance companies after financial authorization and coordinates sourcing, import when required and delivery.

International patients

Medication support before treatment, during hospitalization, after discharge and through the end of the patient’s stay in Israel.

International reach

When the medication is not in Israel, the search does not stop here

Medicare works with an extensive network of suppliers in the United States, Europe, Australia and additional markets. Availability and timing depend on the medication, source, documentation and regulatory requirements.
