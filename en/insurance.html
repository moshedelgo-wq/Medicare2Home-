Medicare

Medicare for insurance companies and insured members

When an insured member needs a medication that is not covered, unavailable in Israel or requires sourcing and import, Medicare coordinates the process with the insurer and patient — from the request and financial authorization through delivery.

One-stop service

When the medication falls outside the standard pathway, Medicare steps in

Medicare works directly with insurance companies. The insurer provides financial authorization for the order, and Medicare handles the subsequent process according to the case.

1

Request intake

Insured member details, prescription and required medication.

2

Medication review

Availability in Israel and, when necessary, sourcing abroad.

3

Authorization & order

Direct coordination with the insurance company and financial authorization.

4

Delivery to the insured member

Coordinated supply according to product requirements.

International supplier network

A solution even when the medication is unavailable in Israel

Medicare has strong sourcing capabilities through suppliers in the United States, Europe, Australia and additional markets. When import is required, Medicare can handle bringing the medication to Israel in accordance with applicable requirements.

Pharmacy team

Professional pharmacist guidance and support throughout the process.

Customer service

A dedicated service line for assistance and coordination.

Three languages

Hebrew, English and Russian.
